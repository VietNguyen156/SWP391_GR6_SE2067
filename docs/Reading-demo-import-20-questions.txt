# PASSAGE 1 — EMAIL
# To: Greenfield Office Services Team
# From: Maya Chen, Operations Manager
# Date: September 8
# Subject: New equipment request procedure
#
# Hello everyone,
#
# Starting September 15, please submit all requests for office equipment through the new
# Facilities Portal. The portal replaces the paper forms that were previously left in the
# supply room. Employees should select "Equipment Request," describe the item, and choose
# either "standard" or "urgent" service. Requests marked urgent must include a short
# explanation and the approval code from the employee's department supervisor.
#
# Standard requests are normally processed within four business days. Urgent requests
# received before 11:00 a.m. are reviewed on the same day, although delivery may take
# another business day. The Facilities team will send an e-mail when an item is ready for
# collection at the east reception desk. Large items, including chairs and printers, will
# be delivered directly to the employee's floor.
#
# During the first week, the old paper forms will remain available for employees who have
# trouble signing in. Please contact Daniel Ruiz at extension 248 if you cannot access the
# portal. Do not send equipment requests to the general help desk; that team handles
# computer and network problems only.
#
# Thank you,
# Maya

1. Why did Ms. Chen write the e-mail?
A. To announce a new procedure for requesting equipment
B. To introduce a new employee to the Facilities team
C. To report a delay in a printer delivery
D. To invite employees to a supply-room meeting
Đáp án: A
Kỹ năng: PURPOSE
Giải thích: The e-mail explains a new portal and process for submitting equipment requests.
Căn cứ: Starting September 15, please submit all requests for office equipment through the new Facilities Portal.

2. When should employees begin using the Facilities Portal?
A. September 8
B. September 15
C. September 18
D. October 1
Đáp án: B
Kỹ năng: DETAIL
Giải thích: The start date is explicitly stated in the first paragraph.
Căn cứ: Starting September 15, please submit all requests for office equipment through the new Facilities Portal.

3. What can be inferred about an urgent request submitted at 1:00 p.m.?
A. It will automatically be rejected.
B. It may not be reviewed until the next day.
C. It will be delivered before the end of that day.
D. It does not require supervisor approval.
Đáp án: B
Kỹ năng: INFERENCE
Giải thích: Only urgent requests received before 11:00 a.m. are reviewed on the same day.
Căn cứ: Urgent requests received before 11:00 a.m. are reviewed on the same day, although delivery may take another business day.

4. In the e-mail, what does "processed" most nearly mean?
A. Repaired
B. Recorded
C. Handled
D. Purchased
Đáp án: C
Kỹ năng: VOCABULARY
Giải thích: A request processed within four days is handled or dealt with within that period.
Căn cứ: Standard requests are normally processed within four business days.

5. Where will employees collect most small equipment items?
A. At the east reception desk
B. In the supply room
C. On the employee's floor
D. At the general help desk
Đáp án: A
Kỹ năng: DETAIL
Giải thích: The Facilities team sends an e-mail when an item is ready at east reception.
Căn cứ: The Facilities team will send an e-mail when an item is ready for collection at the east reception desk.

6. What is the purpose of the old paper forms during the first week?
A. To record deliveries made by the general help desk
B. To help employees who cannot sign in to the portal
C. To request supervisor approval for all purchases
D. To replace the portal permanently
Đáp án: B
Kỹ năng: PURPOSE
Giải thích: The paper forms remain temporarily available as a fallback for sign-in problems.
Căn cứ: During the first week, the old paper forms will remain available for employees who have trouble signing in.

7. What will an employee probably do if unable to access the portal?
A. E-mail the general help desk about equipment
B. Leave a form beside the printer
C. Call Daniel Ruiz
D. Wait until the next month
Đáp án: C
Kỹ năng: INFERENCE
Giải thích: The message directs employees with access problems to Daniel Ruiz.
Căn cứ: Please contact Daniel Ruiz at extension 248 if you cannot access the portal.

8. What does "urgent" most nearly mean?
A. Needed quickly
B. Already delivered
C. Difficult to carry
D. Not approved
Đáp án: A
Kỹ năng: VOCABULARY
Giải thích: Urgent requests receive faster review than standard requests.
Căn cứ: Requests marked urgent must include a short explanation and the approval code from the employee's department supervisor.

9. What can be inferred about the approval process for urgent requests?
A. An urgent request must include authorization from the employee's supervisor.
B. The Facilities team approves every request before it is submitted.
C. Standard requests always require an approval code.
D. Employees may request urgent service without explaining why.
Đáp án: A
Kỹ năng: INFERENCE
Giải thích: The message says urgent requests need both an explanation and a supervisor's approval code.
Căn cứ: Requests marked urgent must include a short explanation and the approval code from the employee's department supervisor.

10. Why does Ms. Chen mention the general help desk?
A. To explain that equipment requests should be sent there
B. To clarify that it handles computer and network problems, not equipment requests
C. To announce that it will close during the first week
D. To ask employees to send their approval codes there
Đáp án: B
Kỹ năng: PURPOSE
Giải thích: The final paragraph distinguishes the help desk's responsibility from equipment requests.
Căn cứ: Do not send equipment requests to the general help desk; that team handles computer and network problems only.

# PASSAGE 2 — CUSTOMER E-MAIL AND SERVICE NOTICE
# To: Jordan Lee
# From: Greenfield Office Services
# Date: October 3
# Subject: Replacement chair delivery
#
# Dear Mr. Lee,
#
# We are writing about your equipment request, number GF-1842. The replacement chair
# originally scheduled for delivery on October 5 will arrive on October 7 because the
# warehouse received the wrong model. We apologize for the inconvenience.
#
# You do not need to submit another request. The delivery team will bring the chair to
# the 4th-floor reception area between 1:00 and 3:00 p.m. on October 7. Please show the
# team the confirmation number in this e-mail. If no one is available to receive the
# chair, it will be taken back to the warehouse and a new delivery date will be arranged.
#
# If October 7 does not work for you, reply to this e-mail by noon on October 4. We can
# arrange delivery on October 9 instead. Please note that the delivery team cannot leave
# large items in an unattended hallway.
#
# Sincerely,
# Greenfield Office Services
#
# SERVICE NOTICE
# The east reception desk will close at 4:30 p.m. on October 7 for a scheduled fire alarm
# inspection. Employees collecting small parcels should arrive before closing. Large
# furniture deliveries will continue to the floor listed on the approved request.

11. Why was the chair delivery delayed?
A. The delivery team could not find Mr. Lee's office.
B. The warehouse received an incorrect chair model.
C. The east reception desk closed early.
D. Mr. Lee submitted a second request.
Đáp án: B
Kỹ năng: DETAIL
Giải thích: The e-mail directly gives the reason for the delay.
Căn cứ: The replacement chair originally scheduled for delivery on October 5 will arrive on October 7 because the warehouse received the wrong model.

12. What can be inferred about Mr. Lee's original request?
A. It was approved and assigned a confirmation number.
B. It was sent to the general help desk.
C. It was for a small parcel.
D. It has already been canceled.
Đáp án: A
Kỹ năng: INFERENCE
Giải thích: The request has an ID and a scheduled delivery; the e-mail says no replacement request is needed.
Căn cứ: We are writing about your equipment request, number GF-1842.

13. What does "arranged" most nearly mean in the e-mail?

Kỹ năng: VOCABULARY
Giải thích: A new delivery date will be organized if the first delivery cannot be completed.
Căn cứ: If no one is available to receive the chair, it will be taken back to the warehouse and a new delivery date will be arranged.
