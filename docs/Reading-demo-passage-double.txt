From: Maya Chen, Operations Manager
Date: September 8
Subject: New equipment request procedure

Hello everyone,

Starting September 15, please submit all requests for office equipment through the new Facilities Portal. The portal replaces the paper forms that were previously left in the supply room. Employees should select "Equipment Request," describe the item, and choose either "standard" or "urgent" service. Requests marked urgent must include a short explanation and the approval code from the employee's department supervisor.

Standard requests are normally processed within four business days. Urgent requests received before 11:00 a.m. are reviewed on the same day, although delivery may take another business day. The Facilities team will send an e-mail when an item is ready for collection at the east reception desk. Large items, including chairs and printers, will be delivered directly to the employee's floor.

During the first week, the old paper forms will remain available for employees who have trouble signing in. Please contact Daniel Ruiz at extension 248 if you cannot access the portal. Do not send equipment requests to the general help desk; that team handles computer and network problems only.

Thank you,
Maya
